2. Our Privacy Principles

All apps built by Mysticode Innovations Inc. follow these core principles:

  • No data collection — We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.
  • No accounts — Our apps do not require sign-up or login.
  • No analytics or tracking — We do not use any analytics services, crash reporting tools, or tracking SDKs.
  • No ads — Our apps are ad-free with no advertising networks.
  • No third-party services — We do not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or social media SDKs.
  • Offline-first — Our apps work entirely offline and make no network requests.

3. Local Data Storage

Our apps store user data (such as progress, preferences, and settings) locally on your device only. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not backed up to any server
  • Is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data

4. Children's Privacy

All MII apps are rated 4+ and are suitable for all ages. Since our apps collect no personal data, they are compliant with COPPA, GDPR Article 8, PIPEDA, Apple App Store guidelines, and Google Play Families Policy. No parental consent is required because no data is collected or transmitted.

5. Device Permissions

Our apps may request limited device permissions (such as notifications for study reminders or audio playback for pronunciation). No permissions involve accessing contacts, camera, location, microphone, photos, health data, or any sensitive information.

6. Data Security

All data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted over any network. The security of your data is governed by your device's built-in security measures, including device lock, disk encryption, and operating system protections.

7. International Compliance

Since MII apps do not collect or process any personal data, no cross-border data transfer occurs. Our apps are compliant with privacy regulations worldwide, including GDPR (EU/EEA), CCPA/CPRA (California), LGPD (Brazil), POPIA (South Africa), and PDPA (Singapore/Thailand).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Effective Date" at the top of this document and in the relevant app store listings. Continued use of our apps after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
